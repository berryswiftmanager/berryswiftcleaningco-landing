What happens next

Three things, and you only have to do one of them.

1

The day before

A temporary authorization hold is placed 24 hours before your visit. You're only charged once the cleaning is complete, and you'll see the hold drop off once the visit is finalized.

2

The morning of

Your housekeeper texts when she's en route, and arrives inside the window we promised. She brings everything she needs.

We don't charge by the hour. She stays until it's done.

3

After she's finished

Walk through whenever you get a minute. That's the one thing we need from you.

If it's not right, we make it right. Let us know within 24 hours and we'll come back to fix it at no charge.

Your details

Your date, your arrival window, and everything you told us about the home are saved on your booking. Call us any time and a real person will pull it up for you.

Need to change something?

No worries at all. Call or text and we'll move your visit, add a room, or pass a note along to your housekeeper. A real person answers.
