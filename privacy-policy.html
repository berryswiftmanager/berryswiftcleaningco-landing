Privacy policy

What we collect, what we do with it, and what we never do with it.

Last updated 20 September 2026

You're handing us your address, your phone number, and a key to your home. We treat all three the same way we treat your house: carefully, and only for what you asked us to do.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we never share your mobile number with anyone for marketing. This page says exactly what we do instead.

1What we collect

We collect what we need to quote your cleaning, get into your home, and reach you about the visit. Nothing beyond that.

  • Your details — name, email address, mobile number, service address or zip code.
  • What you're asking for — the rooms, the square footage, the service type, add-ons, access notes, and anything you tell us about your home.
  • Your text-message consent — which consent box you checked, the date and time, and the page you gave it on.
  • Payment details — handled by our payment processor. We don't store your full card number.
  • Site usage — standard web log information and how the site gets used, gathered in a way that doesn't identify you.

2How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Quote your cleaning and answer your questions.
  • Confirm, schedule, and reschedule your visits.
  • Tell you when your housekeeper is on the way, and check in afterwards.
  • Take payment and send receipts.
  • Send you offers and discounts, when you've asked for them.
  • Make the service better — where something went wrong, we want to know why.

We don't build profiles on you, and we don't sell what we know about you.

3Your mobile number and text messages

The short version

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Your mobile number is not for sale, not for rent, and not for anyone else's marketing list. It's how we tell you your housekeeper is on the way.

What you give us, and how

When you book or ask for a quote, our forms carry two separate consent checkboxes — one for service texts and one for marketing texts. Neither box is checked for you, neither one is required, and you can submit the form with both left blank. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

When you check a box, we record which one you checked, the date and time, and the page you were on. We keep that record so we can show you what you agreed to, and so we only ever send the kind of text you asked for.

What we send

  • Service texts — booking confirmations, appointment reminders, your housekeeper's arrival updates, schedule changes, and a check-in after the clean.
  • Marketing texts — special offers, discounts, and service updates.

Message frequency may vary. Service texts are triggered by your visits, so the number you get tracks how often we clean for you. Marketing texts are occasional and only go out when you've asked for them.

Message and data rates may apply. What a text costs is between you and your mobile carrier. We never charge you for a message.

Stopping them

Reply STOP to any text from us and the texts stop. You'll get one reply confirming you're unsubscribed, and nothing after that. END, QUIT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE all work the same way. Text HELP for our name and how to reach a person, or call (832) 900-3253.

Text delivery depends on your mobile carrier, and carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out doesn't cancel your cleaning — we'll switch to phone and email for your visits. When you'd rather keep the service texts and drop only the marketing ones, tell us and we'll set it that way.

Who touches your number

Your mobile number and your consent record live in our booking and messaging systems, and the only people who see them are the ones on our team who need them to run your cleaning. We use a messaging provider to deliver the texts themselves — it may only send our messages on our instruction, and it is not permitted to use your number for anything else.

Beyond that: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

The consent language itself, in full, is in our terms of service.

4Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we never hand it to anyone for their own marketing.

To actually run the service, a short list of providers processes information on our instruction and only for the job we hired them to do: our booking and scheduling platform, our payment processor, our messaging provider, our email provider, and website analytics. Each one may use your information only to deliver its part of your service.

Two hard limits sit on top of that:

  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We'll also share information where the law requires it. If that ever happens, we'll tell you unless we're barred from doing so.

5Cookies and analytics

We set cookies on your device to give the site the basics it needs to do what you're asking of it. You can clear cookies at any time from your browser settings.

We use Google Analytics to collect standard internet log information and details of how the site gets used — how many visits each part of it gets, and what gets clicked. That information is processed in a way that doesn't identify anyone. We don't try to work out who you are from it, and we don't allow Google to try either.

We also use advertising measurement tools from Google and Meta to see which ads lead to a booking. They tell us that a booking happened, not who you are as a person, and they never receive your text-message consent or anything you've told us about your home.

6How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it for, including our legal, accounting, and reporting obligations. Your data is stored securely in our AWS database.

Your text-message consent record is kept for as long as you're opted in, and for a period afterwards so we can show that you consented and that we honoured your opt-out. Ask us to delete your information and we will, aside from the narrow records the law requires us to hold.

7How we protect it

We take this seriously, and we'd rather be boring about it than clever:

  • Encryption — your personal data is encrypted in storage and in transit.
  • Access control — only the people on our team who need your information to do their job can reach it.
  • Payment separation — card details go to our payment processor, not into our own database.

8Your rights over your information

It's your information. You have the right to:

  • See the information we have about you.
  • Correct or update it.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Opt out of marketing texts, calls, and emails at any time, without losing your cleaning.

To use any of these, email admin@berryswiftcleaningco.com or call (832) 900-3253. A person will handle it.

9Children

Our service is for adults booking a cleaning for their home. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we don't market to children. If you think a child has given us information, email admin@berryswiftcleaningco.com and we'll remove it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect a change in how we work, or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we do, the updated version goes on this page with the date of the revision at the top. When a change affects your text messages or how we share your information, we'll say so plainly rather than leave you to find it.
