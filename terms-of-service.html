1Confirming your booking

Filling out the booking form doesn't lock in the date and time you picked. Once your request is in, we'll call, text, or email you to confirm the visit. Your booking is set when we confirm it, not when you submit the form.

2Your housekeeper

Every housekeeper we send into your home is vetted, insured, and accountable to us for the standard of the work. Depending on the visit, she may work on her own or as part of a team. We'll tell you her name before the day so you know who to expect.

3Text messages and phone calls

When you book, ask for a quote, or hand us your mobile number, you're giving us a way to reach you about your cleaning. Here is exactly what that means, what we'll send, and how you turn it off.

Two kinds of texts, and you choose each one separately

Our quote and booking forms carry two separate consent checkboxes — one for service texts and one for marketing texts. Neither box is checked for you, neither one is required, and you can submit the form with both left blank. We send only what you checked.

  • Service texts are the ones that run your visit: booking confirmations, appointment reminders, your housekeeper's arrival updates, schedule changes, and a check-in after the clean.
  • Marketing texts are special offers, discounts, and service updates.

These are the exact statements you're agreeing to when you check a box:

Marketing texts

I consent to receive marketing text messages, about special offers, discounts, and service updates, from Berry Swift Cleaning at the phone number provided. Message frequency may vary. Message & data rates may apply. Text HELP for assistance, reply STOP to opt out.

Service texts (non-marketing)

I consent to receive non-marketing text messages from Berry Swift Cleaning about booking confirmations, appointment reminders, housekeeper arrival updates, schedule changes, and follow-up after a visit. Message frequency may vary, message & data rates may apply. Text HELP for assistance, reply STOP to opt out.

Saying yes is never a condition of service

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You can book a cleaning with both boxes blank. When you'd rather we stick to phone and email, leave them blank or call us at (832) 900-3253 and we'll book it that way.

Message frequency, and what it costs you

Message frequency may vary. Service texts are triggered by your visits — a confirmation when you book, a reminder the day before, an arrival note on the day, a check-in after. So the number of texts you get tracks how often we clean for you. Marketing texts are occasional and only go out when you've asked for them.

Message and data rates may apply. What a text costs is between you and your mobile carrier, and it depends on your plan. We never charge you for a message.

Reply STOP to opt out. Text HELP for help.

Reply STOP to any text from us and the texts stop. You'll get one reply confirming you're unsubscribed, and nothing after that. END, QUIT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE all work the same way. When you'd rather keep the service texts and drop only the marketing ones, tell us — reply and say so, email admin@berryswiftcleaningco.com, or call (832) 900-3253 — and we'll set it that way.

Text HELP to any of our numbers and you'll get our name and how to reach a person. You can always call (832) 900-3253 or email admin@berryswiftcleaningco.com instead.

Opting out doesn't cancel your cleaning. We'll switch to phone and email for your visits. When you want texts back on later, check the box again the next time you book, or just tell us and we'll turn them back on.

We never share your mobile number

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Our privacy policy sets this out in full.

Delivery

Text delivery depends on your mobile carrier, and carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. When something is time-sensitive and you haven't heard from us, call (832) 900-3253.

Phone calls

We'll also call you about your visits — to confirm a booking, sort out access, or walk through a quote. Tell us to stop calling about offers and we will.

4When we'll leave a visit

We'll leave a visit rather than put your home or your housekeeper at risk. That covers safety concerns, a situation that's inappropriate or uncomfortable, hazardous conditions, and utilities being off — with no power or running water, we can't clean.

If your housekeeper decides the site isn't safe or suitable to work in, she may leave, and our cancellation policy applies.

5Getting your home ready

Please make sure the areas you want cleaned are reachable and ready to work in. If the job needs heavy furniture moved or something hard to reach, tell us before the visit so we can plan the time for it.

6Pets

We're happy to work around your pets, and we'd love to know their names. For their safety and your housekeeper's, if a pet is aggressive or presents a risk, she may leave the site, and our cancellation policy applies.

7Service fees

Your quote is based on standard conditions. If the home turns out to be different from what we quoted — more rooms, more work, or heavier condition than described — we'll call you to talk the price through before we carry on. You'll never find a charge on your card that we didn't discuss with you first.

8Payments and the authorization hold

Payment is due when the cleaning is finished, unless we've agreed something else with you in writing.

We don't charge until the cleaning is complete, but a temporary authorization hold is placed 24 hours in advance. You'll see it drop off once the visit is finalized.

9Our guarantee

If it's not right, we make it right. Let us know within 24 hours and we'll come back to fix it at no charge.

We don't issue refunds. We'd rather send your housekeeper back and finish the job properly than hand you money for a clean home you still don't have.

10Cancelling or moving a visit

We need 24 hours' notice to cancel or move a visit. Without that notice — or if we can't get into your home on the day — a cancellation fee applies, equal to the full price of the visit.

11Breakage and liability

We take real care in your home. We're not liable for damage from normal wear and tear, improper installation, or fragile items you haven't told us about. Your housekeeper carries insurance for damage she causes.

Point out anything delicate before the visit — the wobbly shelf, the heirloom bowl — and we'll work around it or leave it alone.

12Getting into your home

Please make sure we can get in on the scheduled day. If your housekeeper can't get into your home, we'll try to reach you. When we haven't heard back within 30 minutes of her arrival, the visit is skipped and the full fee is charged.

13Hiring your housekeeper directly

When you book with us, you agree not to hire one of our housekeepers or employees directly for similar work within one year of her working in your home.

14Governing law

Any claim arising from or related to this agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, where our services are provided, without regard to its conflict-of-law provisions.

15Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. A change takes effect as soon as it's posted here, and the date at the top of this page tells you when we last touched it. When a change affects your text messages or how we use your information, we'll say so on the page rather than leave you to find it.
